PIPELINE WORKFLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
         APPLIED
            ↓
      SHORTLISTED  ───→  REJECTED
            ↓                ↑
       INTERVIEW   ─────────┤
            ↓                ↑
        OFFERED    ─────────┤
            ↓
         HIRED

Valid Transitions Enforced:
✅ Applied → Shortlisted, Rejected
✅ Shortlisted → Interview, Rejected
✅ Interview → Offered, Rejected
✅ Offered → Hired, Rejected
❌ Any other transition = 400 Bad Request
